有底 Privacy Policy

For 有底 1.6.2 (Build 21) | Last updated 10 November 2026

We built 有底 (InventoryLite) to help solo shop owners, market stall vendors, independent studios, and small sellers track inventory, stock movements, moving-average costs, and gross profits on iPhone without complication. Your business data belongs to you. This privacy policy explains in plain language what stays on your device, how exports and backups work, and how our optional product analytics feature in version 1.6.2 operates.

Business Records You Enter into the App

有底 (InventoryLite) is designed for solo shops, market stalls, independent studios, and small sellers to track inventory, stock-in and stock-out movements, moving-average costs, and gross profit. Depending on how you use the app, you may choose to enter business records such as books, items, variant details, supplier and customer names, stock transactions, quantities, unit prices, notes, and restock alert thresholds.

We encourage you to record only what your business genuinely needs. If you record names or contact details of customers, suppliers, or partners, please ensure you handle and maintain their information lawfully and responsibly.

Where Your Data Lives: Local Storage on Your iPhone

You do not need an account or login to use 有底. The app is built specifically for iPhone running iOS 17 or later (there is no iPad support).

All your core business records—including your ledger books, stock items, cost calculations, profit margins, and notes—are stored primarily in the app's local storage directly on your iPhone. We do not provide or operate any cloud synchronization service for your ledger records, and we do not maintain a remote database containing your books.

The only developer-operated network endpoint in version 1.6.2 is our optional product analytics endpoint, which never receives, processes, or stores any of your business records.

Optional Product Analytics in Version 1.6.2

Version 1.6.2 includes one optional first-party analytics feature created solely to help us understand where users encounter friction or abandon the Pro purchase flow, allowing us to improve the app. It is completely off by default. The app never records, queues, or transmits any analytics data before you explicitly grant consent.

On your first launch of version 1.6.2, a one-time explanation sheet titled "Help Us Improve the App" asks whether you wish to agree or decline. This explanation is shown only once, and if you decline, your choice is remembered. You can change your mind at any time by navigating to Settings > Privacy > Help Improve App.

If you turn the switch off, the app immediately ceases recording events, removes any unsent events queued on your iPhone, and cancels any network request in progress. Turning off or declining analytics will never limit, alter, or revoke any app feature or purchased rights. The app remains fully functional, and you can opt back in at any time.

When enabled, analytics events are transmitted over HTTPS POST to https://a21-funnel.fivechaoice.workers.dev/a21/funnel. This endpoint runs on Cloudflare Workers backed by a Cloudflare D1 database, where Cloudflare processes requests on our behalf. The endpoint returns an HTTP 204 status with no response body. If your device is offline, the app queues events locally (up to 500 events; the oldest are dropped if the limit is exceeded) and transmits them in chronological order once an internet connection is established. Delivered events are immediately deleted from your local queue.

The analytics events we collect are strictly anonymous and are never linked to your personal identity. We do not collect or transmit device identifiers, user identifiers, installation UUIDs, IDFA, IDFV, Apple IDs, email addresses, phone numbers, location coordinates, photos, contacts, microphone audio, or clipboard content. The endpoint does not log or store IP addresses, User-Agent strings, or cookies. We never use analytics data for advertising, cross-app tracking, or cross-site tracking, we do not build advertising profiles, and we never sell your data to third parties.

On our server, we store only aggregated counter rows keyed by the dimension combination: date × event × source × app version × country × country source × free/pro tier × new/legacy cohort × install week. There is no database table for raw events, request payloads, IP addresses, User-Agents, or cookies. A daily automated maintenance task permanently deletes server counter rows older than 13 months. Because server rows consist exclusively of aggregate counts untied to any personal identifier, we cannot isolate, look up, or delete past counts for any individual person. Turning the analytics switch off stops all future collection and purges any unsent data from your device.

Below is the complete whitelist of exact fields that can be sent when you opt in. No other fields are ever transmitted:

  • event: The name of the specific in-app step. Supported events are first_open, product_1, product_5, product_10, product_30, limit_hit_first, pro_seen_first, paywall_open_first, purchase_start_first, purchase_success, purchase_cancel_first, purchase_fail_first, restore_success, consent_shown, consent_yes, paywall_open_total, ret_d7_open, ret_d30_open, ret_d7_record, ret_d30_record, paywall_q_shown, paywall_q_price, paywall_q_notyet, paywall_q_unclear, paywall_q_skip, and review_req. Product count events (product_1/5/10/30) indicate you have recorded that number of products; consent bookkeeping events (consent_shown, consent_yes) are only ever transmitted after consent is granted; paywall_open_total represents an overall heat count rather than a conversion denominator; retention events (ret_d7_open, ret_d30_open, ret_d7_record, ret_d30_record) record only whether the app was opened or a transaction was recorded within the day 7–13 and day 30–36 windows, at most once per install with no transaction details; and paywall question events (paywall_q_shown, paywall_q_price, paywall_q_notyet, paywall_q_unclear, paywall_q_skip) record a one-time question impression and selection when dismissing the paywall, containing no information beyond the selected choice; and the review request event (review_req) records only the number of times the app displays the rating request prompt (a repeatable count), containing no personal information and never uploading any rating content.
  • day: The UTC calendar date when the event occurred, formatted as YYYY-MM-DD. Only the calendar day is transmitted—never an exact time or time zone.
  • app_version: The app version and build number when the event was recorded, for example 1.6.2(21).
  • country: The App Store storefront country code, or the device region code if the storefront code is unavailable.
  • country_src: Indicates the source of the country code, with a value of either "storefront" or "locale".
  • tier: Indicates your current app tier, with a value of either "free" or "pro".
  • cohort: Indicates whether this installation is "new" or "legacy" (reflecting whether the app was used prior to the current version).
  • install_week: The ISO calendar week of the app's first launch after installation, for example 2026-W44. Only the week is transmitted, never the exact day.
  • source: Transmitted solely for Pro-screen and paywall events, with a value of "limit", "insight_entry", "intro", or "settings". This field is omitted for all other events.

In version 1.6.2, our optional anonymous usage statistics introduce two new event categories: retention checks and paywall exit feedback. Retention events record whether the app was opened or a transaction was saved during the day 7–13 and day 30–36 windows, strictly limited to once per install with no ledger details. Paywall feedback events present a single question when a free user cancels the paywall, counting one of four selections to help us understand onboarding friction. Consistent with our privacy principles, these metrics are disabled by default, require your explicit consent, and can be disabled anytime in Settings. They are never linked to personal identity, never used for tracking or advertising, and never collect ledger entries, amounts, products, or device identifiers.

CSV Export, Full Backups, and File Sharing

All core inventory, stock tracking, cost calculation, backup, and export features work locally and offline. You can export your data to CSV spreadsheets or create a full JSON ledger backup whenever you wish.

CSV exports, full backups, and sharing occur solely when you explicitly initiate them using the native iOS share sheet or Files interface. You decide where to save or share your files—such as the Files app, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, or external communication and storage services.

Once an exported file or backup leaves 有底, its storage, transmission, and privacy are governed by the terms and privacy policies of the third-party service or storage provider you selected.

Restoring Data from a Backup

You can restore your ledgers from a previously created full backup file at any time. Restoration is completely under your manual control: the app reads a backup file only when you explicitly select it through the iOS file picker.

To prevent unintended data loss, 有底 always presents a clear confirmation prompt before replacing your local device records with the contents of the chosen backup file.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your local business records remain on your iPhone for as long as you keep the app installed, or until you choose to delete specific transactions, items, or books within the app. If you delete 有底 from your iPhone, iOS permanently deletes the app's local sandbox and all stored records.

Files you have exported or backed up outside the app remain in your chosen destinations (such as the Files app, iCloud Drive, or external drives) until you manually remove them.

For the optional product analytics, server-side aggregate tally rows are automatically deleted after 13 months by our scheduled daily maintenance routine.

No Third-Party Tracking or Advertising

We believe business tools should remain clean and confidential. 有底 does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, third-party behavioral analytics SDKs, or commercial tracking frameworks.

We do not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites, we do not monitor your behavior for advertising purposes, and we never construct advertising profiles from your inventory, costs, or sales data.

The only analytics in 有底 is the optional, first-party product analytics described in this policy, which operates strictly on anonymous event milestones and only after your explicit consent.

Data Security

We protect your business information primarily through our local-first design: because your ledger records remain stored on your iPhone rather than on an external cloud server, they are not susceptible to remote server breaches or centralized database leaks.

Your local app data is protected by iOS's built-in data protection, especially when you set a device passcode. You can also use Face ID or Touch ID to control access. To keep your information safe, we recommend keeping iOS updated, setting a strong passcode, and handling any exported CSV or backup files carefully.

Please note that while we design our systems with security and care, no digital storage medium or electronic transmission method can be guaranteed to be absolutely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this privacy policy to reflect improvements to 有底, operational updates, or changes in applicable legal standards.

If we make changes to how data is handled, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this document, revise our App Store privacy disclosures, and share relevant details in release notes or within the app.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us by email at fivechaoice@gmail.com. We are glad to assist you.

「有底」（InventoryLite）專為日常進銷存管理而設計。在使用過程中，你可能會在 App 內建立各項業務帳目，例如帳本、商品名稱、款式規格、廠商與客戶名稱、進出貨紀錄、交易數量、進貨單價與售價、備註說明，以及庫存警示門檻。

我們建議你僅記錄營運所需的必要資料。若你在帳本中記錄了客戶、廠商或合作夥伴的個人資訊，請務必依循相關法規，妥善且合法地管理與使用這些資訊。

資料儲存位置：你的 iPhone 本機

使用「有底」無須註冊任何帳號，也不需要登入。本 App 專為搭載 iOS 17 或更新版本的 iPhone 設計（未提供 iPad 版本）。

你的所有核心業務紀錄——包括帳本、商品庫存、進出貨紀錄、成本計算、毛利與備註——皆主要保存在你 iPhone 的本機應用程式儲存空間中。開發者並未提供亦未營運任何帳本雲端同步服務，我們也沒有儲存你業務帳目的中央伺服器。

若你關閉此開關，App 會立即停止記錄事件、清空 iPhone 本機上所有排隊待傳送的事件，並取消正在進行中的網路請求。婉拒或關閉此統計開關絕不會影響任何 App 功能或你已購買的權益，所有功能皆維持正常使用，日後亦可隨時重新開啟。

資料安全防護
