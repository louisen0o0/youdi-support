Frequently asked questions

Does 有底 upload my business records automatically?

No. The current version stores your business books, items, transactions and settings primarily on your iPhone. It does not automatically upload those records to a server operated by the developer. Files leave the app only when you choose to export, back up or share them through iOS.

What is the difference between CSV export and a full backup?

CSV is intended for viewing transaction data in spreadsheets or other tools. A full backup is for restoring 有底 data later. CSV does not replace a full backup.

How do I create a full backup?

Open More and choose the full-backup action, then choose a storage location in the iOS interface. Keep a recent backup before changing phones or making major data changes.

How do I restore a backup?

Open More, choose restore, and select a valid backup using the iOS Files picker. 有底 checks the file and asks for confirmation before restoring.

How do low-stock reminders work?

Set a replenishment threshold in item settings. The inventory screen marks items that should be restocked using text and an icon, not colour alone.

Does the current version require an account, contain ads or use subscriptions?

No 有底 account is required. The current version does not include third-party ads or third-party behavioural analytics SDKs. Commercial terms are always those shown on the App Store for the version you download.

常見問題

資料會自動上傳嗎？

不會。目前版本主要將帳本、商品、交易與設定保存在你的 iPhone 本機，不會把這些營運資料自動上傳到開發者自建伺服器。只有你主動匯出、備份或分享時，檔案才會交由 iOS 系統讓你選擇目的地。

CSV 匯出和完整備份有什麼不同？

CSV 適合把交易資料帶到試算表或其他工具查看；完整備份則用於日後恢復 App 資料。CSV 不能取代完整備份。

如何建立與恢復完整備份？

在「更多」中建立完整備份；恢復時透過 iOS「檔案」選擇器挑選有效備份檔。有底會先檢查資料並在你確認後才執行恢復。

庫存不足時怎麼看？

可在商品設定中調整補貨提醒數量；庫存頁會以文字與圖示標示待補貨狀態，不只依賴顏色判斷。

目前有帳號登入、廣告或訂閱嗎？

目前不需要有底帳號登入，也沒有第三方廣告或第三方使用者行為分析 SDK。實際商業方式以 App Store 當下版本頁面為準。
